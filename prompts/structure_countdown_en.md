COUNTDOWN structure (hierarchical enumeration; for lists of criteria, mistakes, or signals):

1. Hook 70/100: how many there are and what it costs to ignore them, without listing any yet.
2. Items in ascending order of impact: each item names a VISIBLE action or object (something that can be shown happening) with its number spoken aloud ("one", "two"...). The number and the data point accompany the action, they don't replace it: on screen they go as an accent over the item's scene, never as their own screen.
3. The final item is the strongest and leads straight into the CTA.
