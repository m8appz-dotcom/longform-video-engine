ARC structure (twist/counterintuitive; for myths, common mistakes, hidden mechanisms):

1. Hook 70/100: the belief EMBODIED IN ACTION — name the action or belief people do/hold (something a camera can show someone doing) that the reel is about to deny. No prior context.
2. First peak 90/100 before 5s: strong contrast, contrarian take, or surprising fact. Don't explain yet.
3. Release: what people believe AND WHAT THEY DO because of that belief (the visible behavior), why it seems logical, what part is incomplete. Not filler: it sets up the next peak.
